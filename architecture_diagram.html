3. Core Pipeline Components

Multimodal Vision OCR Gemini Flash Vision converts raw lab report PDFs directly into structured JSON dictionaries.
Tabular ML Classifier XGBoost ensemble models trained across 10 clinical panels to predict organ-specific risk probabilities.
Explainable AI (XAI) SHAP Shapley Additive Explanations provide individual biomarker attribution scores for complete model interpretability.
Pathology Narrative LLM Gemini LLM prompt chain generates a structured 4-section clinical report including an Action Plan and Risk Assessment.
